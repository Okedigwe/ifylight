About Ify Lights

We sell what we’d rig ourselves.

At Ify Lights Stage and Studio, we believe lighting is the heartbeat of every great performance. From our base in Lagos, we supply professional-grade stage and studio lighting that turns a simple stage into an immersive experience.

Whether you’re lighting a church auditorium, a wedding, a club, a concert or a TV and photo studio, we stock the fixtures, controllers, effects and rigging to make your vision shine, and we’ll tell you honestly what you need.
